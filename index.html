About Us

Estrella Fiesta is a casual restaurant for people who enjoy affordable and creative comfort food. We combine Filipino ingredients and dishes with Mexican serving styles.

Our star logo and our tagline, "Two Cultures, One Flavor.", show how both cultures come together on one plate.

Estrella Fiesta is a fictional restaurant made for a school project. It does not represent an existing registered business.
